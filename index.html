About KMDIA

From Low-Cost Disposables to High-Value Global Innovations

Running a MedTech MSME or startup is a high-stakes race against regulatory hurdles and immense capital needs. KMDIA was established as an independent, non-profit association to turn individual challenges into collective strength.

We are uniting manufacturers, startups, and research institutions to transition Kerala's medical device industry from its traditional strength in low-cost disposables toward high-value, precision-engineered global innovations — making the "Made in Kerala" label an international symbol of quality.

Kerala currently produces a significant share of India's domestic medical devices. With the global market actively seeking cost-effective manufacturing, R&D partnerships, and joint ventures, the opportunity has never been greater — and KMDIA is your launchpad.

Our Core Pillars

Everything Your MedTech Business Needs to Scale

Stop navigating the complex MedTech ecosystem alone. KMDIA's four pillars are designed to reduce operational risks, cut overhead, and accelerate your time to market.

🛡️

Break the Regulatory Bottleneck

Navigate India's complex medical device regulatory landscape with expert guidance — so you spend less time on compliance and more on innovation.

  • CDSCO licensing guidance and process support
  • IVDR / MDR (EU) compliance pathway navigation
  • CE Certification roadmap for export markets
  • Complimentary compliance training for members — drastically reducing advisory costs
🏛️

Government & Procurement Advocacy

Collective voice where individual companies can't go. KMDIA actively lobbies for policies that open direct revenue streams for Kerala manufacturers.

  • Lobbying Kerala State Government for "Make in Kerala" MedTech mandates
  • KMSCL purchase preference advocacy for local devices
  • Representation in national-level policy forums
  • Direct access to government tender pipelines
💰

Unlock Funding Schemes

Capital is the #1 barrier for MedTech MSMEs. KMDIA provides insider navigation of schemes most companies don't even know they qualify for.

  • Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme navigation
  • Central Government R&D and MSME grant facilitation
  • Kerala State incentive scheme guidance
  • Connections to MedTech-focused VC and angel networks
🏭

Infrastructure & Shared Labs

Capital expenditure is the silent killer of MedTech startups. Shared infrastructure is how Kerala competes with larger manufacturing states.

  • Common manufacturing cluster development advocacy
  • Shared testing and Quality Control (QC) facility access
  • Bio-park infrastructure development support
  • Special mentoring and ecosystem support for startups

Why Join KMDIA?

The Kerala MedTech industry is at an inflection point. The global market is actively seeking Indian manufacturing partners — but navigating regulatory hurdles, government schemes, and capital requirements alone is a losing battle for most MSMEs.

KMDIA aggregates your collective bargaining power. One association membership replaces what would otherwise cost lakhs in individual regulatory consulting fees, government liaison work, and industry intelligence.

We work closely with India's premier institutions — SCTIMST, RGCB, IIT Palakkad, Kerala MedTech Consortium (KMTC), and KSUM — to provide frictionless R&D pipelines and connect agile Kerala startups with global industry titans.

For Pre-Revenue Startups: Our platform is specifically designed to be accessible at the earliest stage of your journey. The subsidised fee ensures that capital constraints never prevent you from accessing the support you need.
